2. Finite-parent ordering lemma

Let \(w(X,a)\) be the parent primary obtained from the differential Miura operator,

\[ (a\partial-X_1)(a\partial-X_2)(a\partial-X_3),\qquad w=u_3-\frac a2\partial u_2. \]

For \(V_\lambda=:\!e^{\lambda\alpha_i\cdot\phi}\!:\), a direct parent Wick expansion gives

\[ \operatorname{Sing}[w(z)V_\lambda(w)] =\partial_w\!\left[\left(\frac{B_{i,1}}{z-w}+\frac{B_{i,2}}{(z-w)^2}\right)V_\lambda(w)\right] \]

precisely on the parent screening locus

\[ \boxed{a\lambda+\lambda^2=1.} \]

The unreduced residual factorizes by \(a\lambda+\lambda^2-1\) for both roots. This parent identity is reconstructed independently in independent_check_III_gate3b.py.

Now substitute the two orientations before taking any OPE coefficient:

\[ \begin{array}{lll} {\rm u}:&X=P/\varepsilon+\varepsilon N,&a=\alpha^+/\varepsilon+\varepsilon\alpha^-,\quad \lambda=\varepsilon\mu_{\rm u}(t),\\ {\rm b}:&X=P/\varepsilon-\varepsilon N,&a=\alpha^+/\varepsilon-\varepsilon\alpha^-,\quad \lambda=\varepsilon\mu_{\rm b}(t), \end{array} \qquad t=\varepsilon^2. \]

The transported barred contour reverses orientation. Consequently it contributes a minus sign to the \(+\) contraction and cancels the explicit minus sign in the \(-\) contraction. Coefficient extraction from these two parent normal orders is exactly the Fredenhagen-Hoerl averaged order of their equations (4.29)-(4.31). Because the parent OPE has finitely many contractions and poles, coefficient extraction commutes with the singular-part map. This is the required reduction lemma: no polynomial factor is replaced by a commuting surrogate.

The branch equations are

\[ \begin{aligned} t\mu_{\rm u}^2+(\alpha^++t\alpha^-)\mu_{\rm u}&=1,& t\mu_{\rm b}^2+(\alpha^+-t\alpha^-)\mu_{\rm b}&=1,\\ \mu_3&=-\mu^3-\alpha^-\mu^2,& \bar\mu_3&=-\mu^3+\alpha^-\mu^2. \end{aligned} \]

6. Exact horizontal boundary

Let \(\lambda_i^\pm(t)=\alpha_i\cdot\lambda^\pm(t)\) denote the root component of the source momentum. The exact horizontal section is

\[ \lambda_i^+=\frac12\left(\frac{m-t\mu_{\rm u}^2}{\mu_{\rm u}}+\frac{m-t\mu_{\rm b}^2}{\mu_{\rm b}}\right),\qquad t\lambda_i^-=\frac12\left(\frac{m-t\mu_{\rm u}^2}{\mu_{\rm u}}-\frac{m-t\mu_{\rm b}^2}{\mu_{\rm b}}\right). \]

\[ a_{\rm u}=t\mu_{\rm u}^2+\mu_{\rm u}\lambda_i^++t\mu_{\rm u}\lambda_i^- -m=0, \] \[ a_{\rm b}=t\mu_{\rm b}^2+\mu_{\rm b}\lambda_i^+-t\mu_{\rm b}\lambda_i^- -m=0. \]

For a weight-one parent field the based boundary is

\[ \operatorname{LRes}_{w_0}\partial_w(w^{n+1}V)= \frac{e^{2\pi i a}-1}{2\pi i}\,w_0^{n+1}V(w_0). \]

Since both \(a_{\rm u}\) and \(a_{\rm b}\) vanish as exact formal series, each parent boundary vanishes before taking its coefficient. The odd half-difference therefore obeys
